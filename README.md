## 👋 About

I build multilingual catalogues and the tools behind them: clear product discovery, maintainable content structures, and workflows that show what is ready and what still needs attention.

- **🏗️ Categories as a backbone.** I model catalogues around categories as first-class pages instead of forcing everything into WooCommerce products. Templates stay reusable, navigation stays consistent.
- **🌍 Multilingual by default.** The Boomerang catalogue has Persian, English, Arabic and Russian pages with RTL/LTR layouts.
- **📈 The full path.** Custom PHP and HTML templates through to on-page SEO and analytics — I'd rather own the whole delivery than hand off half of it.
- **⚡ Lean output.** No build tooling where plain HTML/CSS/JS will do the job faster and lighter.
- **🔎 Traceable operations.** Boomerang Commerce OS keeps product jobs, source uploads and prompt versions visible without claiming unfinished work is published.
